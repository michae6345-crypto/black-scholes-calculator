Binomial convergence

CRR price as the step count grows. The even/odd oscillation is real, not a bug: the strike sits at different places in the terminal lattice as the parity of the step count flips.

Monte Carlo convergence

Running estimate from 16 paths on, with a 95% band (±1.96 standard errors). The error shrinks like 1/√n, so ten times more paths buys one more digit, roughly.
